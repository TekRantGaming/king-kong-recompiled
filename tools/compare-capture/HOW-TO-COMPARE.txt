Comparison capture (private test build): ORIGINAL = the Xbox 360 look at 720p, MAX = everything highest at 4K.
1. Set Windows to 3840x2160, then double-click "Start comparison capture.bat" (fullscreen at the desktop size).
2. Play to a good spot. The card at the top says which shot is next and what shows it well.
3. Insert flips the live game between ORIGINAL and MAX (the label at the top left says which one is showing).
4. Home captures: the game pauses, saves ORIGINAL then MAX of the same frame, then resumes (a few seconds).
5. Page Down skips to the next shot, Page Up goes back. A capture moves the card on by itself.
6. Pictures: Documents\king_kong\screenshots\compare\<shot>-<date>-<time>\ original.png, max.png, settings.txt.
7. Taking a shot again keeps the earlier one (a new folder). Home also works inside photo mode (your framing).
8. Edit compare-shots.txt beside king_kong.exe (id | feature | hint) any time, even while playing.
9. Cutscenes cannot be captured (pausing skips them). The HUD and Jack's first-person arms are not in the pictures.
